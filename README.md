# Plant & Simple Planner

I am building a premium digital product for my plant-based food brand, Plant & Simple.



I am attaching our existing Premium Cookbook. STUDY THE ATTACHED COOKBOOK CAREFULLY before designing anything.



The new product I want you to create is a premium printable/digital PDF called:



PLANT & SIMPLE

WEEKLY MEAL PLANNING KIT



Tagline:

Plan. Shop. Prep. Eat Well.



IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a redesign of the cookbook.

It is a complementary meal-planning product that customers can use alongside the cookbook.



The planner should feel like it belongs to the exact same premium brand family as the attached cookbook.



==================================================

1. DESIGN DIRECTION

==================================================



Use the attached Premium Cookbook as the primary visual reference.



Analyze and carry over its:

- typography hierarchy

- editorial layout style

- spacing

- margins

- visual rhythm

- color palette

- typography personality

- illustration/image treatment

- premium food photography style

- paper/editorial feeling

- overall sophistication



Do NOT simply copy pages from the cookbook.



Create a fresh, original planner design that feels like the same brand.



The aesthetic should feel:

- premium

- editorial

- warm

- modern

- sophisticated

- minimal

- practical

- calming

- highly printable



Avoid:

- generic Canva-template appearance

- childish graphics

- excessive decoration

- loud colors

- overly complicated layouts

- corporate spreadsheet appearance

- cheap-looking clipart

- excessive icons

- AI-looking illustrations



The product should look good both:

1. On a phone/tablet/computer

2. When printed at home



Use generous whitespace and clear writing areas.



==================================================

2. BRAND ALIGNMENT

==================================================



Brand:

Plant & Simple



Audience:

People who want plant-based meals to feel easier, more organized, practical, and enjoyable.



The planner must support the customer who already owns or is considering the Plant & Simple Premium Cookbook.



The planner should NOT feel like a generic meal planner that could belong to any food brand.



Make the experience specifically suited to plant-based meal planning.



==================================================

3. PRODUCT STRUCTURE

==================================================



Create a polished planner kit of approximately 10–12 pages.



Every page should have a clear purpose.



PAGE 1 — COVER



Title:

Plant & Simple

Weekly Meal Planning Kit



Subtitle:

Plan. Shop. Prep. Eat Well.



Include a beautiful, editorial plant-based food composition consistent with the cookbook.



Keep the cover elegant and uncluttered.



PAGE 2 — HOW TO USE THIS KIT



Create a short, beautifully designed introduction explaining the simple workflow:



1. Plan your meals

2. Check what you already have

3. Build your grocery list

4. Prep what you can

5. Enjoy your week



Keep the copy concise and encouraging.



PAGE 3 — WEEKLY MEAL PLAN



Create a full Monday–Sunday planning layout.



Include spaces for:

- Breakfast

- Lunch

- Dinner

- Snacks



Also include:

- Week of: ________

- Prep notes

- A small "This week's focus" section



Make enough writing space for real use.



PAGE 4 — WEEKLY MEAL PLAN / SECOND FORMAT



Create a slightly different planning page focused on simplicity.



Include:

- 7 days

- Main meal

- Protein

- Vegetables

- Grain/starch

- Snack



This should help users create balanced plant-based meals without making the page feel like a nutrition spreadsheet.



PAGE 5 — GROCERY LIST



Create a highly organized grocery list divided into:



PRODUCE

- Vegetables

- Fruits

- Fresh herbs



PLANT PROTEINS

- Beans

- Lentils

- Chickpeas

- Tofu

- Tempeh

- Edamame

- Other



GRAINS & STAPLES

- Rice

- Pasta

- Oats

- Quinoa

- Bread/wraps

- Other



PANTRY

- Canned goods

- Sauces

- Spices

- Seeds

- Nuts

- Other



FROZEN

- Frozen vegetables

- Frozen fruit

- Other



OTHER



Each category should have:

- checkbox

- item

- quantity



Do not pre-fill the page with too many products. The customer should be able to write their own list.



PAGE 6 — PANTRY & FRIDGE INVENTORY



Create a clean inventory page.



Sections:

- Pantry

- Refrigerator

- Freezer



Columns:

- Item

- Quantity

- Use soon?

- Notes



Include a small reminder:



"Check what you already have before you shop."



PAGE 7 — PLANT PROTEIN PLANNER



Create a dedicated plant-protein planning page.



Include categories such as:



Beans

Lentils

Chickpeas

Tofu

Tempeh

Edamame

Seitan

Nuts & seeds

Other plant proteins



Include:

- Protein source

- Meal

- Quantity

- Prep needed



The page should help users intentionally include plant protein throughout the week.



Keep the wording practical and avoid making medical or nutritional claims.



PAGE 8 — MEAL PREP PLANNER



Create a weekly meal-prep page.



Sections:

- Prep ahead

- Chop

- Cook

- Wash

- Portion

- Store



Include:

- Task

- Quantity

- When

- Done checkbox



Add a small section:

"Use first"



for ingredients that need to be eaten soon.



PAGE 9 — LEFTOVERS & FOOD-USE PLANNER



Create a smart leftover planning page.



Sections:

- What I have

- Use it in

- Meal idea

- Date



Include a small note:



"Plan around what you already have before buying more."



The goal is to make meal planning feel practical and reduce unnecessary food waste.



PAGE 10 — RECIPE FAVORITES



Create a beautiful recipe collection page.



Fields:

- Recipe name

- Source/page

- Prep time

- Why I love it

- Make again? checkbox



Include enough space to record multiple favorite recipes.



This page should work with recipes from the Plant & Simple Premium Cookbook.



PAGE 11 — WEEKLY PREP CHECKLIST



Create a simple reusable checklist:



□ Review the week's meals

□ Check pantry

□ Check fridge/freezer

□ Make grocery list

□ Shop

□ Wash produce

□ Prep vegetables

□ Prepare proteins

□ Cook grains/starches

□ Portion meals

□ Store leftovers



Add a notes section.



PAGE 12 — MONTHLY MEAL PLANNING / NOTES



Create a flexible monthly planning page.



Include:

- Month

- Weekly meal themes

- Recipes to try

- Ingredients to use

- Favorites to repeat

- Notes



Make this page visually beautiful but highly practical.



==================================================

4. PLANT-BASED CONTENT

==================================================



All examples, food imagery, sample recipes, ingredient references, and visual elements must be plant-based.



Do NOT include:

- meat

- chicken

- fish

- seafood

- dairy

- eggs

- animal-based ingredients



If sample entries are needed, use plant-based examples such as:

- chickpeas

- black beans

- lentils

- tofu

- tempeh

- quinoa

- vegetables

- fruits

- whole grains

- nuts

- seeds



Do not make unsupported health claims.



==================================================

5. PRINT QUALITY

==================================================



Design this as a professional digital PDF product.



Use:

- US Letter as the primary print size

- portrait orientation

- print-safe margins

- high readability

- sufficient writing space

- clean page numbering

- consistent headers/footers



Ensure no important text or elements are too close to the page edge.



The planner must look professional when printed in black and white as well as in color.



==================================================

6. PREMIUM PRODUCT FEEL

==================================================



This is intended to be an optional add-on/upsell to the Plant & Simple Premium Cookbook.



It should feel valuable enough that a customer understands why they would want it in addition to the cookbook.



The cookbook provides the recipes.



The planner provides the SYSTEM for actually organizing and using those recipes.



The customer should be able to:

- choose meals

- plan the week

- organize groceries

- check existing ingredients

- plan plant proteins

- prep ahead

- manage leftovers

- save favorite recipes

- repeat the process every week



Make that distinction clear through the product design.



==================================================

7. COPY STYLE

==================================================



Use concise, warm, intelligent copy.



The tone should be:

- calm

- practical

- encouraging

- premium

- human



Avoid:

- excessive motivational quotes

- generic wellness clichés

- exaggerated health promises

- unnecessary paragraphs



Use short editorial phrases where appropriate.



Examples of the tone:



"A little planning. A much easier week."



"Check what you have. Then make your list."



"Plan meals you'll actually want to eat."



"Keep the favorites. Make room for something new."



Use this style sparingly.



==================================================

8. VISUAL SYSTEM

==================================================



Create a consistent visual system across all pages.



Use:

- consistent typography hierarchy

- consistent section labels

- subtle dividers

- elegant checkboxes

- clean tables

- restrained decorative elements

- carefully placed food imagery



Do not put a large image on every page.



Some pages should be mostly typography and structured writing areas.



The result should feel like a premium editorial workbook rather than a collection of worksheets.



==================================================

9. FINAL DELIVERABLE

==================================================



Create the complete planner as a polished, cohesive product.



Before finalizing:

- Check every page for spelling errors.

- Check that all text fits correctly.

- Check that tables and writing areas are aligned.

- Check that no elements overlap.

- Check that margins are print-safe.

- Check that typography is consistent.

- Check that the design clearly belongs to Plant & Simple.

- Check that all food examples are plant-based.

- Check that the planner feels complementary to the attached Premium Cookbook.



MOST IMPORTANT:

Study the attached cookbook first and make the planner feel like its natural companion.



Do not simply imitate the reference images I may provide.

Build an ORIGINAL Plant & Simple product using the same level of design quality and visual language.
